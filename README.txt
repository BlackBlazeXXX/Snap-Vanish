SNAP VANISH 1.1
Snap your fingers on a video call, turn to dust, and leave an empty room. Snap again to return.

INSTALL (2 minutes)
1. Unzip snap-vanish.zip. Keep the "snap-vanish" folder somewhere permanent (Chrome runs it from there).
2. Open  chrome://extensions  in Chrome (Edge: edge://extensions).
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the "snap-vanish" folder (the one with manifest.json inside).
5. Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin Snap Vanish.
6. Reload any meeting tab that was already open.

TRY IT FIRST (practice room)
1. Click the Snap Vanish icon > "Open practice room" > "Start camera" > allow camera and mic.
2. Wait for "Room: ready". Lean left, then right, so the camera sees more of the real wall behind you.
3. Click "Snap now". Click again to come back.
4. Now try a real snap: hand in view, thumb and middle finger pressed together briefly, then snap.
   Missed snaps? Raise Snap sensitivity. False triggers? Lower it.

USE IT ON A CALL
- Google Meet, Microsoft Teams (web), Zoom in the browser ("Join from your browser"), or WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com in Chrome).
- Turn OFF the meeting app's own background blur or virtual background.
- Check the popup says "Room: ready" before you snap.
- Backup shortcut: Alt+Shift+V (Mac: Option+Shift+V), while the browser is focused.
  Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

BEST-LOOKING EMPTY ROOM
Click "Capture empty room", step out of the picture within 5 seconds, wait for the confirmation.
This uses your real room, so no AI guessing. Without it, the AI fills in whatever the camera
has never seen, which can look soft or slightly odd on detailed backgrounds.
Click "Re-learn room" if you move the laptop or the lighting changes a lot.

GOOD TO KNOW
- The mic is muted by sending silence. The meeting's own mic button will NOT change.
  Do a test call to confirm before you rely on it. The popup shows "muted by Snap Vanish".
- Your own meeting mute stays in charge: if you were muted before, you stay muted after.
- The empty room is a still picture (with faint camera grain). Things that move behind you
  will not move while you are vanished.
- Turning the switch off brings you back, and unmutes, immediately.
- The room needs a few seconds to prepare after the camera starts (longer on slow computers).
- It only covers camera and mic. Screen sharing and your name tile are not affected.

PRIVACY
AI models are inside this folder and run on your computer. The extension uploads nothing and
saves no video. The meeting app still receives your outgoing picture and sound, as on any call.

NOT WORKING?
- "No camera in use": reload the meeting tab, then rejoin.
- Snap ignored: the popup / practice room says why (no click heard, mic unavailable, slow tracking).
- "Saw a snap motion but heard no click": raise sensitivity; check the meeting uses the mic near you.
  Headset noise filtering built into some microphones can hide the click.
- After updating files, press the reload arrow on chrome://extensions, then reload meeting tabs.
